Our Story

Passion Meets Plate

Savor & Sage was born from a simple belief: that exceptional food is a form of storytelling. Founded in 2024 by chef Elara Voss and sommelier Marco Soleil, we set out to create a dining experience that honors both tradition and innovation.

Every dish on our menu tells a story — of the farmer who grew the herbs, the seasons that shaped the harvest, and the artisan techniques passed down through generations. We believe the best meals are ones that connect us to the earth, to each other, and to the moment.
